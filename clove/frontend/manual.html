🚀 1. Getting Started

Quick overview of accounts, navigation, and workspace preferences.
1
Account Setup & Invitation: Sign up with your Name, Email, and Password, or join via an invitation link provided by your workspace Administrator.
2
Navigation Layout: Use the top navigation bar for global search, creating tasks, notifications, and profile settings. Use the left sidebar to navigate between For You (your assigned tasks), Recent, Starred, All Projects, and My Team.
3
Light & Dark Themes: Click your profile avatar in the top-right corner to toggle between Light and Dark mode. Your choice is saved automatically.

📁 2. Projects & Workspaces

Organize your team's initiatives with specialized project views and sprint tracking.
📊 Dashboard View

Real-time Project Health score (0-100), sprint countdown indicators, status distributions, team workload analysis, and overdue task radars.

📋 Kanban Board

Visual workflow columns (To Do, In Progress, In Review, Done). Drag cards freely between columns to transition statuses instantly.

📑 Backlog View

Prioritized flat list of tasks for sprint planning, bulk status updates, and backlog grooming.

📅 Timeline (Gantt)

Visual schedule showing task durations based on start and due dates for milestone and release tracking.

Sprint Dates & Countdown: Click Sprint Dates in the project header to set current sprint start and end dates. CLOVE automatically displays live badges like "3 days left" or "Ends today".

📋 3. Task Management & Kanban Board

Create, prioritize, assign, and track work smoothly.
Creating Tasks

Click the orange + Create button in the top navbar or + New Task in any Kanban column. Enter title, priority, due date, estimated days, and assignee.

Drag-and-Drop

Move task cards across columns. Team members viewing the board will see updates in real-time without needing to refresh.

Filtering Tasks

Filter tasks on the board by Priority (Urgent, High, Medium, Low) or by Assignee to focus on your immediate workload.

Grouping Work

Toggle grouping by Assignee or Priority to review team capacity and identify potential bottlenecks.

💬 4. Team Collaboration & Comments

Communicate effectively within tasks using mentions, attachments, and writing tools.
@
Smart @Mentions: Type @ in the comment input to open user autocomplete. Use arrow keys and press Enter to tag a teammate. Tagged members receive instant notifications.
📎
Attachments & Screenshots: Click the paperclip button to attach images or logs. Image attachments display inline thumbnails with full-size download links.
🪄
Magic Text Improver: Click the wand icon next to any comment or description field to automatically correct spelling, polish punctuation, and capitalize sentences.
🔔
Notifications: The bell icon in the top navbar displays a badge whenever you are assigned a task or mentioned. Click any alert to open the task directly.
